## Why php-s3?

MinIO and Rust/Go S3 alternatives require full root access, dedicated daemons, and custom open ports. Shared hosting gives you none of that.

**php-s3 brings full Amazon S3 compatibility to any shared host** running PHP 8.1+ and MySQL.

  <Card title="Shared Hosting First" icon="rocket">

## Quick Start in 4 Steps

1. **Upload**: Place `index.php`, `.htaccess`, `src/`, and `cli/` in your web directory.
2. **Install**: Open `https://your-domain.example/_admin/install` in your browser.
3. **Connect**: Copy your S3 endpoint, Access Key, and Secret from the Admin Connect panel.
4. **Use**: Configure any standard S3 client in path-style mode.
